# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, product leads, and technical decision-makers evaluating an engineering studio for a web platform, app, AI/ML system, automation, or chatbot build. They arrive comparing studios/agencies and want fast proof of real, shipped work — not promises. Primary job: decide "can this team build what I need, and do they look like they know what they're doing."

## Product Purpose

Brilyx's marketing site converts visitors into qualified leads by demonstrating shipped engineering work across web, apps, AI/ML, automations, and chatbots. Success = a visitor trusts the team's technical credibility fast enough to reach out (WhatsApp click-to-chat) or browse a case study to the end.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, technical. Monochrome, no brand colour — restraint signals engineering rigor over marketing flash. Voice is direct and concrete (real client names, real stacks, real links), never hypey.

## Anti-references

- Flashy agency/Awwwards portfolios: heavy scroll-hijacking, gradient-drenched hero blobs, over-the-top motion for its own sake.
- Generic dev-portfolio template look: identical card grids, uniform icon+heading+text tiles, tech-tag pill soup with no hierarchy.
- Any AI-slop tells: eyebrow labels on every section, em-dashes, gradient text, side-stripe borders.

## Design Principles

1. Restraint is the brand. Monochrome + one neutral accent stays locked across every Work surface — no colour introduced to "liven up" a case study.
2. Proof over promise. Real screenshots, real client names, real live links win over abstract claims or filler copy.
3. Motion is motivated, not decorative. Every reveal/scroll effect must explain hierarchy or sequence; cut anything that's just "looked cool."
4. One layout family, one job. Don't reuse the same section shape back-to-back; vary composition so "Work" doesn't read as a template stamped N times.
5. Technical confidence shows in craft, not claims. Contrast, spacing rhythm, and interaction states are held to the same bar as the client work being shown off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 for large/bold text, visible focus rings, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback for every animation (already partially implemented site-wide).
